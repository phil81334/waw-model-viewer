About WaW Model Viewer

      

        
An unofficial tool made for the World at War modding community.

        

          Browse the stock Modtools models, find a filename by eye, and rotate a
          textured preview. Optional inspection controls show LODs and named
          bones.
        

        

          Colour textures and transparency are shown with simple preview
          lighting, off by default. Hover bones for names or show all names with
          a checkbox. Mesh outline displays the original triangle edges. This
          does not recreate the game's advanced materials or effects. No
          exporting, animation or editing. Game files are never modified.
        

        

          Independent of Treyarch, Activision and UGX. Built with JavaScript,
          Vite, Rust, Tauri and Three.js. Open source under GPL-3.0; see
          ATTRIBUTION.md.
